Ledgy help articles: the other views of a board (table, calendar, timeline, dashboard), My cards on the boards home, and the table and calendar of all boards.

# Boards: views

## board-views-table
title: See a board as a table
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person
ask: board table view; cards as a table; spreadsheet of cards; sort cards by due date; group cards by list; group cards by member; choose table columns; export cards to csv; download board as excel; edit due date in table; change list from table; custom fields in a table
related: board-views-calendar, board-views-timeline, board-views-dashboard

On a board, choose **Table** to see one row per card: its number, title, list, labels, members, start and due dates, checklist progress, each custom field and when it last changed. Only the cards the board's filter shows are listed.

- **Sort**: click a column's heading; click again for the other way, and a third time to go back to the board's order. Cards with nothing in that column always come last.
- **Group by** list, label, member or due status. A card with two labels or two members shows under each.
- **Columns**: show or hide columns and move them up or down. The card's title is always shown. The choice is kept for this board on this computer; **Reset** puts it back.
- **Edit in place** (board admins and members): click a card's list, start or due date, or a custom field to change it; press Enter or click away to save, Escape to cancel. The pencil beside a title renames the card. A date can have a time; leave the time empty for the end of that day. If the change is refused (a start after the due date, or a Ledgerline card that moves only when its record changes), the cell goes back and the reason is shown.
- Click anywhere else on a row to open the card.
- **Export CSV** downloads the rows shown, in the order and with the columns shown, plus each card's due status. It opens in Excel.

The header row and the first columns stay in view while the table scrolls. Observers and closed boards show the table without the editing.

## board-views-calendar
title: See a board on a calendar
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person
ask: board calendar; calendar view of cards; cards by due date on a calendar; drag card to another day; change due date by dragging; cards with no due date; week view of cards; month view of cards; what is due this week on the board; calendar by hour; due time on the calendar; move a card to another hour
related: board-views-timeline, board-views-table, board-views-all-boards, board-planner

On a board, choose **Calendar** for a month or a week (Monday to Sunday) of the cards. Today is circled. Times are IST.

- **Month** shows each card on its day. A card with a start and due date stretches from one to the other; a card with only a start shows **Starts** on its first day. A day with more cards than fit shows **+2 more**: click it to see that week.
- **Week** shows the days by hour. A card due at a time sits at that hour (a card that starts at a time the same day runs from its start to its due time). Cards due on a day with no time, cards that span days and cards with only a start sit in the **All day** row at the top. The red line is now.
- **Drag a card** (board admins and members): to another day to move its dates by that many days, keeping their times; in the week view, onto an hour to give it that due time. A card that has a start moves its start by the same number of days.
- **No dates** lists cards with neither a start nor a due date. Drag one onto a day to give it a due date on that day, or onto an hour in the week view for that day and time.
- The colour at a card's edge and its small mark show its due status: overdue, due within 24 hours, complete, or later.
- Without a mouse, open a card (Enter) and change its dates in the card window.

On a phone the calendar is a list of days with their cards.

## board-views-timeline
title: See a board as a timeline
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person
ask: board timeline; gantt chart of cards; timeline view; swimlanes by member; timeline grouped by label; timeline with no grouping; plan cards over weeks; drag to change start date; extend due date on timeline; cards without dates on timeline; zoom timeline to months
related: board-views-calendar, board-views-table, board-views-dashboard

On a board, choose **Timeline** for a bar per card from its start date to its due date. **Group by** List, Label or Member puts the bars in a row per list, label or member (a card with two labels or two members shows in each); **Nothing** puts them all in one row. A card with only a due date shows a short bar ending on that day; one with only a start, a short bar beginning on it. The blue line is now.

- Zoom to **Days**, **Weeks** or **Months**. **Today** scrolls back to now.
- **Drag a bar** (board admins and members) to move both dates by the days dragged, keeping their times. **Drag its left end** to change the start (a card with only a due date gets a start), or **its right end** to change the due date. A label above the bar shows the new dates as you drag.
- Click a bar, or the title beside it, to open the card.
- **Cards without dates** are listed under the timeline.

## board-views-dashboard
title: Board dashboard: charts of the board's cards
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person
ask: board dashboard; board charts; how many cards are overdue; cards per member; workload by person; cards per list chart; checklist completion; board report; who has the most cards; pie chart of cards; add a chart to the dashboard; change dashboard tiles; cards over time; cards completed per week; line chart of cards
related: board-views-table, board-views-timeline, board-views-my-cards

On a board, choose **Dashboard** for the board's cards in charts. Across the top: how many cards there are, how many are overdue, due within 24 hours and complete. Below are tiles. Each shows one of these:

- **Cards per list**, **per label** or **per member** (a card with two labels or members counts under each);
- **Due status**: overdue, due soon, due later, no due date, complete;
- **Checklist completion**: cards with every item done, some done or none, and the share of all items done;
- **Cards over time**: for each of the last 12 weeks (Monday to Sunday), the cards added to the board and the cards marked complete, as a line. It is worked out from the board's activity; on a very busy board only the newest 25,000 changes are read, and the tile says so.

The first five can be a **bar** chart or a **pie** chart; cards over time is a line. Every chart shows its numbers in words too: the bars and the pie's list give each count and share, and **Show the numbers** under the line opens a table. Hover over a week to see its numbers.

**Change the tiles** (board admins and members): choose **Edit dashboard**, then **Add tile**, choose what each tile shows and Bar or Pie, move tiles earlier or later with the arrows, or remove one. **Default tiles** puts back the first set. **Save dashboard** keeps it on the board for everyone; **Cancel** leaves it as it was. A dashboard has up to 12 tiles.

Click a bar, a slice or a line of a pie's list to see only those cards: the other charts show just them, the cards are listed below to open, and the **Table**, **Calendar** and **Timeline** views show just them too until you click **Show all cards** (or the bar again).

## board-views-my-cards
title: My cards across all boards
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person
ask: my cards; cards assigned to me; what is on my plate; my tasks across boards; my overdue cards; cards due today for me; cards i am a member of
related: board-views-calendar, board-views-dashboard

The boards home shows **My cards**: the open cards you are a member of on every board you can see, by when they fall due: **Overdue**, **Today**, **This week** (to Sunday), **Later** and **No due date**, soonest first. Each shows its board and list, due date, labels and checklist progress; click one to open it.

Cards marked complete are counted, not listed. Archived cards, cards on closed boards and board templates are left out, as are cards on boards you cannot see, even if they name you.

## board-views-all-boards
title: All boards: one table and calendar of every board's cards
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person
ask: all boards table; cards from every board; workspace table; workspace calendar; calendar of all boards; every card due this week; filter cards by board; filter cards by list name; cards across boards by member; cross board view
related: board-views-my-cards, board-views-table, board-views-calendar, board-planner

On the boards home, **Table** and **Calendar** show the open cards of every board you can see together, like Trello's workspace views. Branch staff and authorised persons see only their own boards; nobody sees anyone's Inbox here.

- **Filters**: **Boards**, **Lists** (by name, so "Done" means every board's Done list), **Members**, **Labels** (by name) and **Due** (overdue, due soon, due later, no due date, complete). Your filters are kept on this computer. **Clear filters** shows everything again.
- **Table**: each card with its board, list, labels, members, due and start dates and checklist, soonest due first. Click a heading to sort the page; **View only** marks boards where you look and comment but do not change cards. 500 cards are shown at a time; **Next** and **Previous** page through the rest.
- **Calendar**: a month, or a week by hour, of the cards' dates, as on a board's own calendar. Change dates from the card window or the board's calendar.
- Click a card to open it on its board.

Closed boards, board templates, archived cards and mirror cards are left out. When there are very many cards, choose some boards to see them all.

## board-views-map
title: Why boards have no map view
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Admin
ask: board map view; show cards on a map; map of card locations; trello map; card location map
related: board-views-calendar, board-views-table

Boards have no map view. A map needs an internet map and address-lookup service, which would send the locations on the firm's cards outside the firm. A card's **Location** is kept as text on the card, and the **Table** view can show and sort it alongside the other details.
