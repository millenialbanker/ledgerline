Ledgy help articles: Boards, the Trello-style lists of cards for any piece of work, and the Ledgerline boards that follow Ledgerline's own records.

# Boards

## boards-overview
title: Boards and who sees them
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: what are boards; trello board; kanban board; make a board; create a board; new board; who can see my board; private board; firm wide board; share a board; add someone to a board; board members; board admin; board observer; close a board; delete a board; copy a board; star a board; ledgerline boards; watch a board; eye on the board
related: boards-lists-cards, boards-card-window, boards-filters-search, boards-permissions

[[boards|Boards]] hold lists of cards, like Trello: one board for month-end close, an audit, a new branch, anything with steps and people.

- **Create board** on the boards home: give it a name, choose who can see it, a background colour and its starting lists (To do, Doing, Done unless you change them). You are its admin.
- **Firm-wide** boards are open to everyone at head office. People who are not members can read them and (unless the board's settings say otherwise) comment; members change lists and cards. When the admins allow it, people who are not members see **Join board** in the board's header.
- **Private** boards are seen only by their members. Branch and partner (AP) staff keep their boards private, and see only boards they were added to.
- **Share** on the board's header adds people and sets each one's role: **admin** (settings, members, close and delete), **member** (lists and cards) or **observer** (reads and comments). A firm Admin is an admin of every board they can see. The board's **Settings** say who may comment and who may add members.
- **Ledgerline boards** follow Ledgerline's own records (for example the work queue or reconciliation breaks). Their lists are the records' stages; a card moves when its record changes, and a move Ledgerline cannot make is refused with the reason.
- **Star** a board to keep it at the top of the home. The board's **⋯** menu has About, **Watch**, Activity, Archived items, Labels, Settings (visibility, permissions, covers, background) and Copy board. An eye beside the board's name means you watch it. A board admin can **Close** a board (it keeps its cards and can be reopened) and then **Delete** it.

## boards-lists-cards
title: Lists and cards
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: add a card; add a list; rename a list; move a card; drag a card; move a card to another list; reorder lists; card limit; wip limit; list colour; sort a list; archive a card; archive a list; restore an archived card; delete a card; move all cards; card cover; card number; copy a list; duplicate a list; copy list to another board; move list to another board; move a list between boards; collapse a list; expand a list; hide a list; narrow list; watch a list; mark a card complete from the board; tick a card done; round checkbox on a card; restore an archived list; search archived items; find an archived card; where did my list go
related: boards-card-window, boards-shortcuts, boards-overview, boards-notifications

- **Add a card**: **Add a card** at the foot of a list, type the title and press Enter. The box stays open for the next card; Esc closes it.
- **Add a list**: **Add another list** at the right end of the board.
- **Move cards and lists** by dragging them. On a phone or tablet, press and hold a card to pick it up. Without a mouse, open the card's **⋯** (quick edit) and choose **Move**, then the board, list and position. A Ledgerline board may refuse a move; the message says why and the card goes back.
- **Quick edit**: a card's **⋯**, a right-click on the card, or **e** changes it without opening it (see **Quick edit, undo and redo**). Most changes show **Undo** for a few seconds.
- **Rename a list** by clicking its name. The list's **⋯** menu adds a card, copies or moves the list, moves all its cards to another list, sorts it (due date, title, newest, oldest), watches or collapses it, sets a colour, sets a **card limit**, moves the list left or right, archives all its cards, or archives the list.
- **Copy list…** makes a copy on this board (under a new name) or on another team board you can change. Its open cards come with their checklists as they are, labels (on another board, matched by name and colour and made there if missing), dates, custom fields and the members who are on that board; not comments or attachments. Cards linked to a Ledgerline record stay with the record.
- **Move list to another board…** takes the list and its open cards to another team board. The cards get that board's next numbers; links to the old cards still open them in their new place. Archived cards stay behind, and the old list shows in Archived items as moved. Lists on Ledgerline boards cannot be copied or moved, and nothing moves onto one.
- **Collapse** (the arrows by a list's name, or the list's menu) shrinks a list to a narrow strip with its name and card count; click the strip to open it again. It is your own choice, kept for you on every device.
- **Watch** in a list's menu tells you when a card is added to the list or moved into it. An eye by the list's name means you watch it.
- A **card limit** is a soft limit: when a list holds more cards, its count shows as, say, 5/4 in orange. Cards are never refused.
- The card's front shows its labels (click one to show or hide label names), number, title, due date (grey; orange when due within a day; red when overdue; green when complete), and whether it has a description, comments, attachments, checklist items done, votes and members.
- **Mark complete from the front**: point at a card with a due date (or Tab to it) and tick the round box by its title. A complete card keeps its green tick; on a phone or tablet the box is always there. You need to be able to change the card.
- **Archive** hides a card or list without losing it. **Archived items** in the board's menu restores them (a card whose list is archived needs the list back first), with a search box for both cards and lists; a board admin can delete an archived card for good.

## boards-card-window
title: The card window
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: open a card; card details; card description; checklist on a card; add a checklist; tick checklist item; assign checklist item; due date on a card; reminder before due date; mark card complete; attach a file to a card; attach a screenshot; paste an image into a card; card cover image; comment on a card; mention someone on a card; react to a comment; join a card; watch a card; vote on a card; copy a card; link to a card; convert checklist item to card; turn a checklist item into a card; copy checklist items from another card; reuse a checklist; @board; @card; mention everyone on the board; mention the card's members; label colours; create a label; edit a label; subtle label; bold label; label shades
related: boards-lists-cards, boards-notifications, boards-shortcuts, boards-permissions

Click a card to open it. The address in the browser then points at the card, so you can copy the link (**Share** → card link, or **Copy link** in quick edit) and send it to a colleague who can see the board.

- **Title**: click it to change it. **in list …** under it moves the card.
- **Members**, **Labels** and **Dates** sit at the top; **Add to card** on the right adds them. Dates have a start, a due date with an optional time (IST) and a reminder; members and watchers are told when the reminder time comes. Tick the box by the date to mark the card complete.
- **Description**: the toolbar above the box adds bold, italic, strikethrough, headings, lists, quotes, code, links, mentions and emoji; you can also type the markdown yourself (see **Formatting descriptions and comments**). A link to another card shows as a chip with its number, title and list. Ctrl+Enter saves.
- **Checklists**: add as many as you need; tick items, click one to edit it, give it a person or a due date, and reorder by dragging or with the up and down buttons. **Hide checked items** keeps the list short; the bar shows how much is done. **Copy items from…**, when you add a checklist, copies the items of any checklist on any card of the board. The card button on an item (**Convert to card**) makes it a card of its own just below this one, with the item's person and due date; the item leaves the checklist and the activity says so.
- **Labels**: **Labels** picks the card's labels. A board admin can **Create a new label** or edit one (the pencil) there: a name and one of 30 colours, each of the ten in a subtle, normal and bold shade. **All the board's labels** lists them with how many cards use each.
- **Attachments**: **Attachment** uploads files (up to 15 MB each) or attaches a link. You can also drop files on the window or paste a screenshot. An image can be the card's **cover**; **Cover** also offers colours and a full-card cover.
- **Comments**: type @ and a name to mention someone (they are told); **@card** tells the card's members and watchers, and **@board** (from the board's admins and members) everyone who is an admin or member of the board; on a Ledgerline board, everyone whose role works it. An observer's @board is plain text. Nobody is told twice about one comment. Edit or delete your own; react with 👍 👀 ✅ 🎉 ❤️ 😂 🙏 ❗. **Show details** mixes in the card's activity.
- **Actions**: Move, Copy (choose to keep checklists, labels and members), Watch, Archive, and **Share** (the card's link, print, export and its email address); a board admin can delete an archived card.
- Observers see everything but cannot change the card; they can still watch and vote, and comment and react unless the board's settings keep comments to members.

## boards-filters-search
title: Filter cards and search every board
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: filter cards; my cards; cards assigned to me; overdue cards; cards due this week; cards with no members; filter by label; search cards; find a card; search all boards; find card by number; clear filters
related: boards-shortcuts, boards-lists-cards

- **Filter** on the board's header narrows the cards shown: a keyword (words on the card, its #number, a label or a person), members (including **No members** and **Cards assigned to me**), due dates (no dates, overdue, due in the next day, week or month, marked complete or not), labels (any of those picked, or all of them), checklists (all done, in progress, not started, none) and lists. The button shows how many filters are on; **Clear filters** removes them. Other views of the board show the same filtered cards.
- **Search cards** at the top looks through every board you can see: titles, descriptions, comments and checklist items, or **#12** for a card number. Pick a result to open the card.

## boards-notifications
title: Board notifications
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: board notifications; bell on boards; who mentioned me; i was added to a card; card reminders; why was i told about this card; stop notifications for a card; unwatch a card; mark notifications read; watch a board; watch a list; stop watching a board; notifications for a whole board; told about every card on a board
related: boards-card-window, boards-overview, boards-lists-cards

The bell on the boards screens shows how many notifications you have not read. You are told when someone adds you to a board or a card, mentions you, gives you a checklist item, or moves or comments on a card you watch, and when a card's reminder time comes.

- You watch the cards you make or comment on, and cards you are a member of. **Watch** on a card starts or stops watching it.
- **Watch a list** (its ⋯ menu) to hear when a card is added to it or moved into it. **Watch a board** (the board's ⋯ menu) to hear when a card on it is added, moved, marked complete, commented on or given a new due date. An eye by the name shows what you watch.
- One change brings you one notice: if you are on the card, watch it or are mentioned, you get that notice and not a second one for watching the list or board.
- Click a notification to open its card; **Mark all as read** clears the count.
- The open board refreshes itself every few seconds while it is on screen, without closing an open card or losing what you are typing.

## boards-shortcuts
title: Keyboard shortcuts on boards
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: board keyboard shortcuts; trello shortcuts; boards hotkeys; keyboard on boards; shortcut to add a card; how to use boards without a mouse; select a card with the keyboard; arrow keys on a board; switch board shortcut; toggle labels with number keys; copy and paste a card
related: boards-quick-edit, boards-adding-cards, boards-lists-cards

Shortcuts work when you are not typing in a box. Press **?** on a board to see them all.

- **Moving around**: the arrow keys, or **h j k l**, select a card (up and down a list, or across lists, passing over collapsed lists); the selected card has a blue ring. **Enter** opens it. **b** switches to another board, **/** searches cards on every board, **w** shows or hides the board menu, **Esc** closes a menu, window or composer, or clears the selection.
- **On the board**: **n** adds a card to the list under the pointer, opening it first if it is collapsed (or to the first open list), **f** opens the filter, **q** shows only cards assigned to you, **x** clears filters, **;** shows or hides label names, **z** undoes your last change and **Shift+Z** redoes it.
- **On the selected card** (or the card under the pointer, or the open card): **e** quick edit, **t** edit the title, **1** to **0** add or remove the board's first ten labels (in the order of the board's labels), **d** dates, **m** members, **s** watch, **v** vote, **c** archive. In an open card **l** opens labels and **Space** joins or leaves.
- **Ctrl+C** (**⌘C** on a Mac) copies the selected card; **Ctrl+V** pastes a copy into the list under the pointer, or after the selected card, on this or another board in the same browser tab. The copy keeps the checklists, members and (on the same board) labels. Cards are not pasted onto Ledgerline boards: their cards are Ledgerline's own records.
- While typing: **Ctrl+Enter** saves a description or comment; in a description **Ctrl+B** bold, **Ctrl+I** italic, **Ctrl+K** link.
- Every button, list and card can be reached with Tab. Quick edit moves or copies a card without dragging; a list's **⋯** menu moves the list left or right.

## boards-quick-edit
title: Quick edit, undo and redo
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: quick edit a card; edit a card without opening it; right click a card; rename a card from the list; card menu on the board; undo on a board; undo a card move; undo archive card; i archived a card by mistake; restore a card i just archived; undo label change; redo on a board; why can't i undo
related: boards-shortcuts, boards-lists-cards, boards-card-window

**Quick edit** changes a card from the board. Open it with the card's **⋯**, a right-click on the card, or **e** on the selected card (or the card under the pointer).

- The title becomes a box: type and press **Enter** (or **Save**) to rename the card; **Esc** closes quick edit without saving.
- Beside it: **Open card**, **Edit labels**, **Change members**, **Change cover**, **Edit dates**, **Move**, **Copy**, **Copy link**, **Mirror** (to another board) and **Archive**. Each opens the same choices as the card window.
- Observers (and everyone on a closed board) see only **Open card** and **Copy link**. A card that follows a Ledgerline record cannot be renamed or archived here: its title and list come from the record. A mirror's title and dates are changed on the original.
- On a phone, quick edit opens from the bottom of the screen.

**Undo**: after a change made on the board screen, a message with **Undo** appears for a few seconds, and **z** undoes your last change on that board (**Shift+Z** redoes it). Undo works for moving a card (dragging too), archiving a card or a list, labels, members, dates, the title, the description, marking a card complete, moving all cards in a list, sorting a list and archiving all its cards.

- Each browser tab keeps its own list of the last 30 changes for each board; it is gone when the page is reloaded.
- Undo puts the card back as it was before your change. If someone has changed the same thing since, it says the card was changed since and changes nothing, so their work is not overwritten.
- Moves of cards on a Ledgerline board are not undone: they act on Ledgerline's own records. Move the card back, or work the record in Ledgerline. Moves to another board are not undone either; move the card back from that board.

## boards-adding-cards
title: Add cards quickly: labels, people, several lines and files
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: add a label while adding a card; hashtag label in card title; add a member while adding a card; add card at the top of a list; add many cards at once; paste a list of tasks; paste several lines into a card; one card per line; drop a file on a list; make a card from a file; drag a file onto the board
related: boards-lists-cards, boards-shortcuts, boards-quick-edit

In a list's **Add a card** box:

- **#** adds a label: type **#** and pick one from the list, or type its name (**#DP billing**, **#urgent**; spaces can also be written as _ or -). A label without a name goes by its colour (**#green**).
- **@** adds a member: **@farhan** or **@farhan.khan**. They are told they were added.
- **^** sets the card's place: **^top**, **^bottom** or a position such as **^3**.
- These words are taken out of the title, so "Fix bill #DP billing @farhan ^top" makes the card "Fix bill" at the top of the list, with the label and the member. Words that match no label or person stay in the title.
- **Pasting several lines** asks "Make N cards, one per line?". **Make N cards** adds one card for each line (up to 100, each with its own #, @ and ^); **Make one card** puts the lines into the box as one title.
- **Files dropped on a list** (not on a card) make a new card named after the first file, with every file attached (up to 15 MB each). Files dropped on a card are attached to it.

## boards-formatting
title: Formatting descriptions and comments
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: format a card description; bold text in a card; heading in a card description; table in a card description; code block in a card; strikethrough on a card; link to another card; card link chip; emoji in a comment; emoji shortcodes on boards; numbered list in a description; quote in a comment
related: boards-card-window, boards-shortcuts

The toolbar above a card's description adds bold, italic, strikethrough, a heading, bulleted and numbered lists, a quote, code, a link, a mention and emoji. You can also type it yourself; descriptions and comments show the same way:

- Two stars on each side make text **bold**; `_italic_` and `~~strike~~` work the same way, and back-ticks show `code`.
- A line starting `#`, `##` or `###` and a space is a heading; `>` a quote; `-` a bulleted list; `1.` a numbered list; `---` on its own a dividing line.
- Three back-ticks on a line of their own start and end a block of code, shown exactly as typed.
- A table: a line of headings between pipes, a line of dashes, then the rows, for example `| Exchange | Trades |`, `|---|--:|`, `| NSE | 120 |`. A colon on the right of the dashes aligns that column right; on both sides, centre.
- Links: a web address, or `[text](https://…)`. A link to a card (from **Copy link** or **Share**) shows as a chip with the card's number, title and list when you can see that card; otherwise it stays a plain link.
- Emoji: the smiley on the toolbar, or a short code such as `:tada:`, `:white_check_mark:`, `:warning:` or `:thumbsup:`.
- **@** and a name mentions someone. In a comment they are told; in a description it only shows their name.
- Everything is shown as text: no web page code runs, and links go only to web addresses and to Ledgerline's own screens.

## boards-permissions
title: Board permissions: commenting, members and joining
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: board permissions; who can comment on a board; turn off comments on a board; disable commenting; only members can comment; observers cannot comment; who can add members to a board; let members add people; members can invite; join a board; join board button; anyone at head office can join; how do i join a board
related: boards-overview, boards-card-window

A board's admins set its permissions in **Settings** (the board's **⋯** menu). Ledgerline holds to them, whatever screen or tool is used.

- **Commenting**: **Disabled** (no one comments), **Members** (the board's admins and members) or **Members and observers**, the setting every board starts with. On a firm-wide board the observers are everyone at head office. Reactions follow the same setting; writers can always delete their own comments.
- **Adding and removing members**: **Admins** (the start) or **All members**. With all members, a member can add people as members or observers and remove anyone but an admin; only admins change roles or add admins.
- **Anyone at head office can join** (firm-wide team boards): people who are not members see **Join board** in the board's header, which makes them a member. The board's activity records who joined. Branch and partner (AP) staff do not see firm-wide boards, so they cannot join them.
- **Ledgerline boards** take their members from people's roles, so only commenting can be set there.
