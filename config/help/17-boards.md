# Boards

## boards-overview
title: Boards and who sees them
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: what are boards; trello board; kanban board; make a board; create a board; new board; who can see my board; private board; firm wide board; share a board; add someone to a board; board members; board admin; board observer; close a board; delete a board; copy a board; star a board; ledgerline boards
related: boards-lists-cards, boards-card-window, boards-filters-search

[[boards|Boards]] hold lists of cards, like Trello: one board for month-end close, an audit, a new branch, anything with steps and people.

- **Create board** on the boards home: give it a name, choose who can see it, a background colour and its starting lists (To do, Doing, Done unless you change them). You are its admin.
- **Firm-wide** boards are open to everyone at head office. People who are not members can read them and comment; members change lists and cards.
- **Private** boards are seen only by their members. Branch and partner (AP) staff keep their boards private, and see only boards they were added to.
- **Share** on the board's header adds people and sets each one's role: **admin** (settings, members, close and delete), **member** (lists and cards) or **observer** (reads and comments). A firm Admin is an admin of every board they can see.
- **Ledgerline boards** follow Ledgerline's own records (for example the work queue or reconciliation breaks). Their lists are the records' stages; a card moves when its record changes, and a move Ledgerline cannot make is refused with the reason.
- **Star** a board to keep it at the top of the home. The board's **⋯** menu has About, Activity, Archived items, Labels, Settings (covers, visibility, background) and Copy board. A board admin can **Close** a board (it keeps its cards and can be reopened) and then **Delete** it.

## boards-lists-cards
title: Lists and cards
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: add a card; add a list; rename a list; move a card; drag a card; move a card to another list; reorder lists; card limit; wip limit; list colour; sort a list; archive a card; archive a list; restore an archived card; delete a card; move all cards; card cover; card number
related: boards-card-window, boards-shortcuts, boards-overview

- **Add a card**: **Add a card** at the foot of a list, type the title and press Enter. The box stays open for the next card; Esc closes it.
- **Add a list**: **Add another list** at the right end of the board.
- **Move cards and lists** by dragging them. On a phone or tablet, press and hold a card to pick it up. Without a mouse, open the card's **⋯** and choose **Move**, then the board, list and position. A Ledgerline board may refuse a move; the message says why and the card goes back.
- **Rename a list** by clicking its name. The list's **⋯** menu adds a card, moves all its cards to another list, sorts it (due date, title, newest, oldest), sets a colour, sets a **card limit**, moves the list left or right, archives all its cards, or archives the list.
- A **card limit** is a soft limit: when a list holds more cards, its count shows as, say, 5/4 in orange. Cards are never refused.
- The card's front shows its labels (click one to show or hide label names), number, title, due date (grey; orange when due within a day; red when overdue; green when complete), and whether it has a description, comments, attachments, checklist items done, votes and members.
- **Archive** hides a card or list without losing it. **Archived items** in the board's menu restores them; a board admin can delete an archived card for good.

## boards-card-window
title: The card window
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: open a card; card details; card description; checklist on a card; add a checklist; tick checklist item; assign checklist item; due date on a card; reminder before due date; mark card complete; attach a file to a card; attach a screenshot; paste an image into a card; card cover image; comment on a card; mention someone on a card; react to a comment; join a card; watch a card; vote on a card; copy a card; link to a card
related: boards-lists-cards, boards-notifications, boards-shortcuts

Click a card to open it. The address in the browser then points at the card, so you can **Copy link** and send it to a colleague who can see the board.

- **Title**: click it to change it. **in list …** under it moves the card.
- **Members**, **Labels** and **Dates** sit at the top; **Add to card** on the right adds them. Dates have a start, a due date with an optional time (IST) and a reminder; members and watchers are told when the reminder time comes. Tick the box by the date to mark the card complete.
- **Description**: plain text with **bold** (two stars either side), _italic_ (underscores), `code` (back-ticks), links and lists (lines starting with a dash or a number). Ctrl+Enter saves.
- **Checklists**: add as many as you need; tick items, click one to edit it, give it a person or a due date, and reorder by dragging or with the up and down buttons. **Hide checked items** keeps the list short; the bar shows how much is done.
- **Attachments**: **Attachment** uploads files (up to 15 MB each) or attaches a link. You can also drop files on the window or paste a screenshot. An image can be the card's **cover**; **Cover** also offers colours and a full-card cover.
- **Comments**: type @ and a name to mention someone (they are told); edit or delete your own; react with 👍 👀 ✅ 🎉 ❤️ 😂 🙏 ❗. **Show details** mixes in the card's activity.
- **Actions**: Move, Copy (choose to keep checklists, labels and members), Watch, Archive, Copy link; a board admin can delete an archived card.
- Observers see everything but cannot change the card; they can still comment, react, watch and vote.

## boards-filters-search
title: Filter cards and search every board
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: filter cards; my cards; cards assigned to me; overdue cards; cards due this week; cards with no members; filter by label; search cards; find a card; search all boards; find card by number; clear filters
related: boards-shortcuts, boards-lists-cards

- **Filter** on the board's header narrows the cards shown: a keyword (words on the card, its #number, a label or a person), members (including **No members** and **Cards assigned to me**), due dates (no dates, overdue, due in the next day, week or month, marked complete or not), labels (any of those picked, or all of them), checklists (all done, in progress, not started, none) and lists. The button shows how many filters are on; **Clear filters** removes them. Other views of the board show the same filtered cards.
- **Search cards** at the top looks through every board you can see: titles, descriptions, comments and checklist items, or **#12** for a card number. Pick a result to open the card.

## boards-notifications
title: Board notifications
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: board notifications; bell on boards; who mentioned me; i was added to a card; card reminders; why was i told about this card; stop notifications for a card; unwatch a card; mark notifications read
related: boards-card-window, boards-overview

The bell on the boards screens shows how many notifications you have not read. You are told when someone adds you to a board or a card, mentions you, gives you a checklist item, or moves or comments on a card you watch, and when a card's reminder time comes.

- You watch the cards you make or comment on, and cards you are a member of. **Watch** on a card starts or stops watching it.
- Click a notification to open its card; **Mark all as read** clears the count.
- The open board refreshes itself every few seconds while it is on screen, without closing an open card or losing what you are typing.

## boards-shortcuts
title: Keyboard shortcuts on boards
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: board keyboard shortcuts; trello shortcuts; boards hotkeys; keyboard on boards; shortcut to add a card; how to use boards without a mouse
related: boards-lists-cards, boards-filters-search

Shortcuts work when you are not typing in a box. Press **?** on a board to see them.

- On a board: **n** adds a card to the list under the pointer (or the first list), **f** opens the filter, **q** shows only cards assigned to you, **x** clears filters, **/** searches cards, **Esc** closes a menu, window or composer.
- In a card: **d** dates, **l** labels, **m** members, **c** archive or restore, **Space** joins or leaves the card, **Ctrl+Enter** saves a description or comment.
- Every button, list and card can be reached with Tab. A card's **⋯** menu moves or copies it without dragging; a list's **⋯** menu moves the list left or right.
