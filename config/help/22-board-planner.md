# Boards: Inbox, Planner and calendar feeds

## board-inbox
title: Your Inbox: jot cards down fast
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: inbox; trello inbox; add a card quickly; quick capture; jot down a task; private card; my own list; press i; add to inbox; move inbox card to a board; who can see my inbox
related: board-planner, board-views-my-cards, board-inbox-review, boards-overview

Everyone has an **Inbox**: a private place to jot cards down before you know which board they belong on. It is on the boards home, beside the Planner, and the first time you use it Ledgerline makes it for you.

- **Add a card**: type in the box at the top of the Inbox (on the home or in the Planner) and press Enter, or press **i** anywhere on the boards screen for **Add to Inbox** (you can give a due date there too).
- Inbox cards are full cards: open one for dates, checklists, a description, files and comments. You can plan time for them in the Planner.
- **Move** a card to any board and list where you can add cards. It leaves your Inbox with its dates, checklists, comments and files; labels and custom fields belong to a board, so they are left behind.
- **In normal use only you can see your Inbox**: not other staff, not automation. It is not on the boards home with the other boards, its notifications come only to you, and only you can be on its cards. To share a card, move it to a board.
- Your Inbox is still part of the firm's records, which the firm must produce at an inspection. The compliance officer or an Admin can open it **read-only**, giving a reason; this is recorded in the audit trail and you get a notice saying who opened it and why. Keep client instructions on the boards where the team works them, not only in your Inbox.

The Inbox has one list and stays open. Automation rules do not run on it, it cannot be a template, and it has no calendar feed of its own (its cards with due dates are in your **My cards** feed).

## board-planner
title: Planner: plan your week by hour
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: planner; plan my week; time block; block time for a card; schedule a card; my week by hour; drag a card onto my calendar; plan time for a task; set as due date from planner; trello planner
related: board-inbox, board-views-my-cards, board-views-calendar, board-calendar-feeds

On the boards home, **Planner** shows your week (Monday to Sunday) by hour, in IST:

- **All day**: your cards due that day with no time, or starting that day.
- **Due times**: your cards due at a time show as a small flag at that hour.
- **Plan blocks**: time you set aside for a card. They are yours alone and do not change the card.

Your cards are the cards you are a member of, on every board you can see, and your Inbox.

**Plan time** for a card: drag it from the side (your **Inbox**, and **Your cards** with no time planned this week) onto a day and hour. It gets 30 minutes. Or press **Plan** beside it and choose the day, start and length; this works without a mouse and on a phone.

**Change a block**: drag it to another hour or day, or drag its bottom edge to make it longer or shorter (15-minute steps). With the keyboard, select the block and use the up and down arrows to move it 15 minutes, left and right to move it a day, Shift with up or down to change its length, and Delete to take it off the plan. Click it or press Enter for more: **Open the card**, **Change the time**, **Set as due date** (the end of the block becomes the card's due date, if you can change the card) and **Take off the plan**.

A block stays with its card when the card moves from your Inbox to a board. If you can no longer see a card, its block shows as a grey block you can remove.

Ledgerline's own calendar (Calendar & circulars) is the firm's obligations by role, not a calendar of yours, so it is not shown in the Planner.

## board-calendar-feeds
title: See your cards in Google Calendar or Outlook
screen: boards
roles: Ops maker, Ops checker, Risk manager, Compliance officer, Branch staff, Authorised person, Admin
ask: calendar feed; ical feed; ics; subscribe to my cards in google calendar; outlook calendar of cards; calendar sync; trello calendar sync; webcal; switch on calendar feeds; stop a calendar feed; feed address leaked; calendar feeds india-only mode; why can't i switch on calendar feeds
related: board-planner, board-views-my-cards, board-views-calendar

A calendar feed puts cards' due dates into Google Calendar or Outlook. On the boards home choose **Calendar feeds**; for one board, choose **Calendar feed** in the board's menu.

**Off until the firm's Admin switches them on.** Card titles can name clients, and a feed copies them out of Ledgerline to the calendar service and to anyone that calendar is shared with. The Admin switches feeds on for the whole firm, or for single boards from the board's menu, and confirms a warning first. Switching feeds off empties every feed the next time a calendar reads it.

- **My cards**: the cards you are a member of on every board you can see, and your Inbox. Needs feeds on for the firm.
- **A board**: every card on that board with a due date. Needs feeds on for the firm or for that board.

**Get address** makes a long, secret address. It is shown once; Ledgerline keeps only a scrambled copy, so if you lose it, make a **New address** (the old one stops working). In Google Calendar choose **Other calendars**, **+**, **From URL**; in Outlook choose **Add calendar**, **Subscribe from web**. Calendars read it again on their own schedule, usually every few hours.

What a feed holds: each card's due date as an event (all day when it has no time; 30 minutes when it has one), the card's title (with a tick when complete), its board and list, and a link back to the card. Never its description, comments, checklists or files. Cards due more than about four months ago are left out.

**India-only mode**: when the server runs in India-only mode, calendar feeds cannot be switched on, and feeds made before are empty calendars. A calendar service such as Google Calendar or Outlook fetches a feed and keeps it outside India, and SEBI's cloud framework expects regulated data to stay in India. The feed settings say so, and Security lists calendar feeds under where data goes.

**Stop** a feed and its address stops working at once. A feed also stops when you can no longer see the board, or your sign-in is switched off. Making, stopping and switching feeds are kept in the audit log.

## board-inbox-review
title: Open a person's Inbox for an inspection or investigation
screen: boards
roles: Compliance officer, Admin
ask: open someone's inbox; read a staff member's inbox; inbox for inspection; produce inbox records; compliance access to inbox; see another person's inbox; investigate an inbox; audit an inbox
related: board-inbox, boards-overview

Inboxes are private in normal use, but they are part of the firm's records: staff may jot client instructions there, and an exchange or SEBI inspection, a complaint or an investigation may need them. The **compliance officer** or an **Admin** can open a person's Inbox read-only.

On the boards home choose **Open a person's Inbox**, choose the person and type the reason (at least a few words). **Open read-only** shows every card in it, archived ones too, with its description, checklist, comments, the names of its files, and the Inbox's activity (which shows cards moved to boards, and where).

- The opening, the person and your reason go to the **audit trail**, sealed against change.
- The owner gets a notice: "<your name> (Compliance) opened your Inbox: <reason>".
- It is read-only: you cannot move, change or comment on the cards, and the Inbox does not appear among your boards or in your search.
- Branch staff, authorised persons and other roles cannot open another person's Inbox.
